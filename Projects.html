A Selection of Our Engineering Projects

From industrial water treatment to HVAC, fire protection and piping networks — a look at installations GTE has designed and delivered across Algeria.

PROJECT 01

HCA Ain Naadja Military Hospital

Caloriffuge HVAC PlomberieFire Protection VRD Loop Irrigation Algieria / Ain Naadja
 

Our team successfully delivered comprehensive HVAC, Thermal Insulation (Calorifuge), Plumbing, Fire Protection VRD Loop, and Irrigation works for the Ain Naadja Military Hospital, ensuring the highest standards of quality, safety, and operational reliability. The project involved the installation of energy-efficient HVAC systems, complete plumbing infrastructure, high-performance thermal insulation for mechanical networks, an external Fire Protection VRD Loop to provide reliable firefighting water distribution across the site, and a modern irrigation system designed to efficiently maintain the hospital's landscaped areas. Every phase of the project was executed with precision, using premium materials and in full compliance with international engineering standards, delivering durable and efficient mechanical infrastructure for one of Algeria's most important healthcare facilities.

PROJECT 02

Marriotte Trust real estate bab ezzouar

cvc insulation PlomberieFire Sprinkler System Irrigation Algieria / Bab zouar
 

Our team successfully executed a complete range of mechanical engineering works for the Marriott Trust Real Estate project, delivering high-quality installations that meet international standards for safety, performance, and long-term reliability. Scope of Work HVAC (Heating, Ventilation & Air Conditioning) Design and installation of high-performance HVAC systems to ensure optimal indoor comfort, energy efficiency, and reliable climate control throughout the facility. The works included chilled water piping, ventilation networks, equipment installation, balancing, testing, and commissioning. Thermal Insulation (Calorifuge) Supply and installation of premium thermal insulation for chilled water, hot water, and mechanical piping systems. The insulation minimizes energy loss, prevents condensation, improves system efficiency, and ensures compliance with international insulation standards. Plumbing Complete installation of domestic water supply and drainage networks, including stainless steel, PPR, PVC, and HDPE piping systems. The project covered sanitary installations, water distribution, pressure testing, commissioning, and quality assurance for reliable long-term operation. Fire Protection – Sprinkler System Installation of a complete automatic fire sprinkler system designed in accordance with international fire safety standards. The scope included sprinkler piping networks, valves, fittings, fire protection equipment, pressure testing, flushing, and system commissioning to ensure maximum protection of the facility and its occupants. Project Highlights Complete mechanical installation services High-quality materials and certified equipment Compliance with international engineering standards Energy-efficient and sustainable system design Precision installation and rigorous quality control Comprehensive testing and commissioning Reliable, safe, and long-lasting performance This project demonstrates our expertise in delivering integrated mechanical solutions for premium hospitality and commercial developments, ensuring exceptional quality, operational efficiency, and long-term reliability.
